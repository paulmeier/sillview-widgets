# Market Series

Overlay one or more market series on one chart — two or more compare as growth of $10k.

- **Category:** Market
- **Type:** `market-series` (first-party built-in)
- **License:** MIT

## Configuration

- `series` (string | string[]) — required: One market series id, or an array of ids to overlay (e.g. ["spy","agg"]).

This widget's implementation ships compiled into the sillview app. Installing it from the
marketplace simply makes it available to add to a dashboard.
